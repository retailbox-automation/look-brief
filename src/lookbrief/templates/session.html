How we read your inspiration

YouCam Makeup Transfer puts the makeup from your inspiration photo on your face. We read the colours it leaves in each zone, then build your look from ready-made shades of your palette. The look above is that recipe, rendered by YouCam Makeup VTO.

How far is the recipe from your inspiration?

Your look is a recipe of ready-made shades, chosen to look good on your skin. It is not a copy of the transfer, so a difference here is expected. We show it so you can see how far the recipe moved from the photo you love.
